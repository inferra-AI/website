Start deploying with Inferra

Connect your repository, use your monthly free allowance and redeem an eligible campaign code in your wallet.

Your monthly free allowance

The first $5 of usage is free every month per account. A card is required.

Reserve resources within your allowance, or buy prepaid credits for more capacity.

Get started and redeem a code

  1. Sign up and install the Inferra GitHub App.
  2. Add a card and open Wallet → Redeem code.
  3. If you have an offer code, enter it and confirm. The wallet checks eligibility and shows the credits added; amounts and availability come from the offer terms.
  4. Connect your repository and follow the setup guide.

Codes can expire or have eligibility rules. The wallet confirms redemption; this landing page cannot validate a code.

Pay for what you reserve from prepaid credits: CPU $16.19/vCPU-month, memory $1.78/GB-month, storage $0.104/GB-month. Postgres backups are $0.0299/GB-month, free for accounts that never bought credits. The first $5 of usage is free every month per account; a card is required. The bill cannot grow beyond what you prepaid.

Trial, Paid and resource quotas

Start with $15 credit for up to 14 days, card required. Paid is $25/month including $25 of usage; additional usage is billed during the month.

Trial

Start with $15 of trial credit or 14 days, whichever comes first. Card required; one trial per card. Stripe verifies your card without charging it. Unused trial credit expires. Trial apps use Spot machines. You can run apps, native Postgres, native Valkey and S3 buckets; no other AWS services.

Native databases are rolling out. Confirm availability with support@inferralab.com before relying on them. A trial change is refused only if it would use the remaining credit in under a day.

When the trial ends, apps stop and native services scale to zero. Data, config and URLs are kept for 30 days. Subscribe to Paid within that window and everything resumes automatically. After 30 days everything is deleted; your repository config is never changed.

Paid: $25/month including $25 of usage

Paid is $25/month including $25 of usage. Usage beyond that is extra; unused included usage does not roll over. The paid month starts when you subscribe, with the full $25 charge and full $25 of included usage. Any trial credit is dropped. No annual billing. Available to USA customers only for now.

Production: $20/vCPU-month, $10/GiB-month memory and $0.15/GiB-month disk. Development apps on Spot: $10/vCPU-month and $5/GiB-month memory; disk stays $0.15/GiB-month. Usage is priced per resource, per hour.

Development branches can opt into on-demand machines at production rates. Native databases are charged once per service file at on-demand rates, however many branches attach. AWS service prices are shown separately; SQS and DynamoDB on-demand are coming soon and are not sold yet.

Example: 1 vCPU and 2 GiB memory running for a full production month uses $40 before disk. Your $25 includes $25 of that usage, so the total is $40. With $18 of usage, you pay $25. A development Spot app at 1 vCPU and 2 GiB uses $20 before disk.

When your card is charged

Usage beyond the included $25 is billed mid-month and at month-end. The month-end charge includes the next $25 subscription payment as a separate line in the same card charge. Your billing month runs from your subscribe date, not the calendar month.

If the unbilled total since the last charge reaches $100, it is charged immediately, at most once a day. Every charge settles everything unbilled up to that moment. The $100 threshold is locked for the first 3 months; after that, only support can arrange a change. It is not a YAML setting.

Daily usage cap

There is no monthly spending limit. The default daily usage cap is $100 per UTC day. At the cap, running apps keep running, but new apps, new services and cost-adding changes are blocked; metered AWS access pauses for the rest of the day. The cap resets at 00:00 UTC. For a higher cap, email support@inferralab.com. The daily cap and the $100 unbilled charge threshold are separate controls.

If a payment fails

On days 0–7, payments are retried and everything keeps running, but cost-adding changes are refused. Emails are sent on days 0, 3 and 6. On day 8, apps stop. RDS and ElastiCache are snapshotted and deleted; RabbitMQ and Kafka are deleted and their messages are lost. S3, SQS, DynamoDB and native disks are kept.

Paying during days 8–30 restores service automatically: databases from snapshots, RabbitMQ and Kafka recreated empty. Everything is deleted on day 30. The dashboard shows the deletion date; reminder emails go out 7 days and 1 day before.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel and service runs until the end of the paid month, followed by a 30-day hold and then deletion. Export your data before the hold ends. Usage already used is not refundable. The $25 subscription payment is refundable on request within 7 days if usage was under $5. A card dispute stops the account. For cancellation, payment details, invoices or a refund, use Account → Billing or email support@inferralab.com.

Resource quotas are separate

Set branch resource quotas in .inferra/repo.yaml; set account and repo allocations in account.yaml at the root of the private <account>/.inferra repository. Provide cpu, memory and storage together. Quotas are resource limits, not charges or a monthly budget. Each preview adds usage. Account services shared by previews are charged once per service file and share data.

Check usage and deployment

Review account usage and billing in the dashboard. Use inferra usage for resource usage and inferra deployments APP for deployment progress. See pricing and setup.

Data processing agreement

How we handle personal data that's inside the apps you run on Inferra. It forms part of our terms.

Roles

For personal data in your apps, databases and buckets (“customer data”), you are the controller and we are the processor. For your own account and billing details, we're the controller; the Privacy policy covers that.

What we process

Subject matterHosting, running, backing up and supporting your apps on Inferra
DurationWhile your account is open, plus the 7-day deletion window
Kinds of dataWhatever your apps store or handle; you decide
Whose dataYour users, customers, staff and anyone else whose data your apps hold
What we do with itStore, run, back up, restore and delete it, only to provide Inferra

Your instructions

We process customer data only on your documented instructions: these terms and how you configure your apps. If we think an instruction breaks data-protection law, we'll tell you. We don't look inside customer data except to fix a problem you've asked us to fix, or where the law requires it.

Confidentiality

Everyone at Inferra who can access customer data is bound to keep it confidential, and access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

Security

Our measures are on the security page and include: every app isolated with gVisor; a private network per branch; databases reachable only by apps bound to them; secret values encrypted (AES-GCM) and write-only; Postgres point-in-time restore for 7 days. We don't hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification yet.

Each subprocessor is bound by data-protection terms at least as protective as these, and we stay responsible for them. We'll give 30 days' notice before adding one. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds and we can't resolve it, you can close your account and get a refund of unused credits.

Helping you

We'll help you answer requests from people whose data you hold, and with data-protection impact assessments and regulator questions, as far as our role allows. Contact support@inferralab.com.

Breaches

If we learn of a breach affecting customer data, we'll tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, with what we know, what we're doing about it, and updates as we learn more.

Audits

We'll give you the information you reasonably need to show you're meeting your obligations. Where that isn't enough, you or an auditor you choose (bound by confidentiality) may audit us once a year with 30 days' notice, at your cost, in a way that doesn't disrupt the service or other customers.

Deletion and return

You can export your data at any time (see Leave anytime). When you close your account or delete a service, we keep data for 7 days so mistakes can be undone, then delete it, including backups as they age out. We only keep data longer where the law requires it.

International transfers

Customer data is processed in the United States. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (controller to processor, Module 2) and the UK Addendum, which are part of this agreement.

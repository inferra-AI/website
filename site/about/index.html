About

Bring your code. Every branch you choose runs live, with its own database and files, on a bill you prepaid.

Inferra is a cloud for small teams that would rather ship than run a platform team.

Why we built Inferra

We've been the team that ships the product and runs the platform underneath it, at the same time. We know what that week looks like.

Building the app is hard enough. Then came everything around it: one account for the API, another vendor for Postgres, another for file storage, another for preview environments, and a pipeline gluing them together with secrets copied by hand. Every new branch meant setup work. Every new teammate meant another round of access requests.

And none of it stayed done. Servers needed patching, databases needed upgrades, certificates expired, and alerts arrived at odd hours. Keeping the infrastructure running became a second job next to building the product.

Then the bills. Not one bill but several, each priced in its own units, each able to grow on its own. We learned what a job stuck in a loop costs from the invoice, after the fact.

We kept asking the same question: why does running an API, a worker and a database need a platform team?

So we built the platform we wanted. Connect a repo, add two short files, push. We keep the servers, HTTPS certificates and patches up to date, so you don't have to. Every branch you choose runs live in its own private namespace, with its own database and files. You pay from prepaid credits for exactly what you reserve, so a runaway job can't spend more than you bought.

We're a small, independent team, building in the open: status, prices and limits on the page, and what isn't done yet listed next to what is.

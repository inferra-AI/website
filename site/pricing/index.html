Pricing questions

How does the free usage work?

The first $5 of usage is free every month per account. A card is required. Pay for what you reserve from prepaid credits after the free usage.

Can the bill surprise me?

Your bill cannot grow beyond what you prepaid. You choose your reservations and buy credits. The bill can't surprise you.

What do backups include?

Managed Postgres has nightly backups and continuous logs. Restore to any point in the past 7 days. Postgres backup storage is $0.0299 per GB-month, and free for accounts that never bought credits.

What do the calculators include?

The estimates include reserved CPU, memory and storage, before Postgres backup storage. A vCPU is a unit of computing power. GB means gigabytes. Monthly estimates use 730 hours.

How are services priced?

Reserve CPU and memory for apps, managed Postgres and cache. Cache uses Valkey, is Redis-compatible, runs in memory only and has no backups. S3 buckets are priced by reserved size and private by default. Services using your own Docker image can have an optional disk and are not backed up.

Charges, caps and retention

Extra usage is billed mid-month and at month-end, with the next $25 subscription payment at month-end in the same card charge. If the unbilled total reaches $100, it is charged immediately, at most once a day. Every charge settles everything unbilled. The threshold is locked for the first 3 months; after that only support can arrange changes.

The $100/day usage cap is separate from the charge threshold. It resets at 00:00 UTC. Running apps keep running at the cap; new cost-adding changes are blocked and metered AWS access pauses. There is no monthly usage limit.

After trial expiry, data, config and URLs are kept for 30 days; subscribe within that window to resume automatically. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month, followed by a 30-day hold, then deletion. If a payment fails, everything runs on days 0–7 while retries occur and cost-adding changes are refused; on day 8 apps stop and service-specific recovery limits apply. Everything is deleted on day 30. Read the complete payment failure terms.

Usage used is not refundable. Request a refund of the $25 within 7 days if usage was under $5. A dispute stops the account. Questions: support@inferralab.com.

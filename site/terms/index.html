Who we are and this agreement

Inferra is run by [Inferra Labs, Inc.], [1000 N King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801] (“we”). “You” means the person or organisation using Inferra. If you sign up for an organisation, you confirm you're allowed to accept these terms for it.

You accept these terms when you create an account or use Inferra. They include our Privacy policy, Refunds policy and, for personal data in your apps, our Data processing agreement. Together they're the whole agreement between us.

Your account

You sign in with GitHub. You're responsible for what happens under your account and for keeping your GitHub account secure. You must be at least 18, and the information you give us must be accurate. Inferra isn't for children.

Trial and payment

Start with $15 of trial credit or 14 days, whichever comes first. Card required; one trial per card. Paid is $25/month including $25 of usage. Additional usage is extra; unused included usage does not roll over. Stripe verifies the trial card without charging. Unused trial credit expires. Paid starts on the subscribe date with the full $25 charge and included usage; trial credit is dropped. USA customers only for now. No annual billing.

Additional usage is charged mid-month and at month-end. The month-end card charge also includes the next $25 subscription payment as a separate line. An unbilled total of $100 is charged immediately, at most once a day; each charge settles everything unbilled. The threshold is locked for the first 3 months; after that only support can arrange changes. Prices are in US dollars; taxes apply where required. Stripe processes payments. See pricing and refunds. A card dispute stops the account.

The default daily usage cap is $100 per UTC day. At the cap, running apps keep running; new cost-adding changes are blocked and metered AWS access pauses until 00:00 UTC. There is no monthly spending limit.

When service stops

When the trial ends, apps stop and native services scale to zero. Data, config and URLs are kept for 30 days. Subscribe to Paid within that window to resume automatically. After 30 days everything is deleted; repository config stays untouched.

If a card fails, retries continue on days 0–7 and everything keeps running, but cost-adding changes are refused. On day 8 apps stop. RDS and ElastiCache are snapshotted then deleted; RabbitMQ and Kafka are deleted and their messages are lost. S3, SQS, DynamoDB and native disks are kept. Paying during days 8–30 restores databases from snapshots and recreates RabbitMQ and Kafka empty. Everything is deleted on day 30. See the full schedule.

Acceptable use

Don't use Inferra to:

  • attack, scan or overload other systems, including ours
  • get around quotas, billing or isolation
  • host content that exploits or harms children
  • run systems where a failure could cause death or serious injury, or that need HIPAA compliance

Report abuse or copyright problems to support@inferralab.com.

Suspension

We may pause or remove workloads that break these rules, threaten the security or stability of Inferra, or that the law requires us to stop. Where we can, we'll warn you first and give you time to fix it; in an emergency we'll act first and tell you why straight after. We'll restore service once the problem is fixed.

Your content and responsibility

Your code, data and apps stay yours. You give us only the permission we need to build, run, back up and show them to you. We don't use your code or data to train AI models.

You're responsible for your apps and what they do. If someone makes a claim against us because of your apps or content, you'll cover our reasonable costs of dealing with it.

Other services

Inferra connects to services we don't control, such as GitHub, Stripe and any external APIs your apps call. Their own terms apply to them.

Changes to Inferra

We improve Inferra all the time and may change or remove features. If we remove something you rely on, we'll give reasonable notice. If we ever shut Inferra down, we'll give at least 30 days' notice. Refunds follow the refund policy.

Availability

We work hard to keep Inferra running and publish live status at status.inferralab.com, but we don't offer a service-level agreement yet. Postgres can be restored to any point in the last 7 days; caches and app disks aren't backed up, so keep your own copies of anything critical.

Ending the agreement

To close your account, email support@inferralab.com from your account's address. Cancel and service runs until the end of the paid month, then a 30-day hold, then deletion. Export data before the hold ends. Usage already used is not refundable; request a refund of the $25 within 7 days if usage was under $5. See Leave anytime for taking your data with you.

We may end your account for serious or repeated breaches of these terms, or if the law requires it, with notice where we can. Refund requests follow the refund policy. Sections that by their nature should continue, such as payment, liability and governing law, survive the end of the agreement.

Nothing here limits liability that can't be limited by law, or your rights as a consumer.

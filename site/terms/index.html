Who we are and this agreement

Inferra is run by [Inferra Labs, Inc.], [1000 N King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801] (“we”). “You” means the person or organisation using Inferra. If you sign up for an organisation, you confirm you're allowed to accept these terms for it.

You accept these terms when you create an account or use Inferra. They include our Privacy policy, Refunds policy and, for personal data in your apps, our Data processing agreement. Together they're the whole agreement between us.

Your account

You sign in with GitHub. You're responsible for what happens under your account and for keeping your GitHub account secure. You must be at least 18, and the information you give us must be accurate. Inferra isn't for children.

Prepaid credits and payment

You pay for the CPU, memory and storage you reserve, plus metered Postgres backups, from credits you buy in advance. Your spending can't go past your balance. Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes, which we add where the law requires. Stripe processes payments.

Rates are on the pricing section. We'll give 30 days' notice of price changes; they never apply to usage that has already happened. Credits are used in the order they were bought, can't be transferred, and have no cash value except as described in Refunds. If you dispute a charge with your bank, we treat it as a refund request and may pause the account while it's open.

Every account gets $5 of usage free each month while a valid card is on file. The allowance isn't cash, doesn't carry over and can't be refunded.

When credits run out

At $0, new deploys stop at once. After a 3-day grace period, your apps pause. Your data is kept for 7 days from the day the apps pause; adding credits in that window brings the apps and data back. After that, paused data may be deleted.

Acceptable use

Don't use Inferra to:

  • break the law, infringe anyone's rights, or break export-control or sanctions rules
  • mine cryptocurrency
  • send spam or run phishing, malware or botnets
  • attack, scan or overload other systems, including ours
  • get around quotas, billing or isolation
  • host content that exploits or harms children
  • run systems where a failure could cause death or serious injury, or that need HIPAA compliance

Report abuse or copyright problems to support@inferralab.com.

Suspension

We may pause or remove workloads that break these rules, threaten the security or stability of Inferra, or that the law requires us to stop. Where we can, we'll warn you first and give you time to fix it; in an emergency we'll act first and tell you why straight after. We'll restore service once the problem is fixed.

Your content and responsibility

Your code, data and apps stay yours. You give us only the permission we need to build, run, back up and show them to you. We don't use your code or data to train AI models.

You're responsible for your apps and what they do. If someone makes a claim against us because of your apps or content, you'll cover our reasonable costs of dealing with it.

Other services

Inferra connects to services we don't control, such as GitHub, Stripe and any external APIs your apps call. Their own terms apply to them.

Changes to Inferra

We improve Inferra all the time and may change or remove features. If we remove something you rely on, we'll give reasonable notice. If we ever shut Inferra down, we'll give at least 30 days' notice and refund all unused purchased credits.

Availability

We work hard to keep Inferra running and publish live status at status.inferralab.com, but we don't offer a service-level agreement yet. Postgres can be restored to any point in the last 7 days; caches and app disks aren't backed up, so keep your own copies of anything critical.

Ending the agreement

To close your account, email support@inferralab.com from your account's address. We stop billing, refund your unused purchased credits, and delete your data 7 days later. See Leave anytime for taking your data with you.

We may end your account for serious or repeated breaches of these terms, or if the law requires it, with notice where we can. If we end it for a breach, unused credits are refunded unless the breach was fraud or abuse. Sections that by their nature should continue, such as payment, liability and governing law, survive the end of the agreement.

Liability

Inferra is provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim, and we're not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or data you could have backed up. Nothing here limits liability that can't be limited by law, or your rights as a consumer.

We'll give at least 30 days' notice of material changes to these terms, by email or in the dashboard.

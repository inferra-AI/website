Who we are

[Inferra Labs, Inc.], [1000 N King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801], is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact support@inferralab.com. Personal data inside the apps you run on Inferra is covered by the DPA instead.

The GitHub App reads secret names, never their values. Values reach Inferra only when you add them in the dashboard or through a GitHub workflow you set up.

Cookies

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or session replay. If we turn on visitor analytics, it will be first-party and cookieless, and we'll update this page first. The dashboard uses one sign-in cookie to keep you logged in; it isn't used for tracking.

How long we keep it

  • App logs: 1 day.
  • Account data: until you close your account, then 7 days.
  • Deleted services: 7 days.
  • Support emails and issues: up to 2 years.
  • Billing and tax records: as long as tax law requires, usually 7 years.

Who we share it with

Only the subprocessors listed in the DPA (AWS, Stripe, GitHub, Zoho), professional advisers, and authorities when the law requires it. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Where it's processed

In the United States. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, get a copy to take elsewhere, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email support@inferralab.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Inferra is for people aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children. If you believe a child has given us data, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll post changes here and, for important ones, tell you by email or in the dashboard.

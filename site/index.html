Questions

Questions about running your app.

What counts as a worker?

A background worker is a process with no public web address, such as a queue consumer or scheduler. Set run.process: worker in your branch file to run one. It stays on and restarts if it crashes.

Can my app call services outside Inferra?

Yes. Apps reach databases and APIs anywhere on the public internet over verified TLS. Allowlist our fixed outbound IP addresses (shared by all apps) and keep credentials in secrets. How to connect, including TLS certificates →

Can I run MongoDB or RabbitMQ?

Yes. Bring your own Docker image as a service, with environment settings and secrets. Those services aren't backed up; choose managed Postgres if you need backups and restore to any point in the last 7 days.

What happens when credits hit $0?

New deploys pause at once. After a 3-day grace period, apps pause. Your data is kept for 7 days, and adding credits brings everything back. Your card is only charged when you buy credits.

How do I leave?

Whenever you like. Your code is already in your repo; copy your database with pg_dump and your files with any S3 tool. Leave anytime →

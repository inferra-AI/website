Questions

Questions about running your app.

What counts as a worker?

A background worker is a process with no public web address, such as a queue consumer or scheduler. Set type: worker in app.yaml to run one. It stays on and restarts if it crashes.

Can my app call services outside Inferra?

Yes. Apps reach databases and APIs anywhere on the public internet over verified TLS. Allowlist our fixed outbound IP addresses (shared by all apps) and keep credentials in secrets. How to connect, including TLS certificates →

Can I run MongoDB or RabbitMQ?

Managed RabbitMQ is available on Paid when enabled. Custom image services such as MongoDB are not available yet. You can connect to an external database over verified TLS.

What happens when the trial ends?

Apps stop and native services scale to zero. Data, config and URLs are kept for 30 days. Subscribe to Paid within that time and everything resumes automatically. After 30 days everything is deleted; your repository config stays untouched. See payment failure and cancellation terms.

How do I leave?

Whenever you like. Your code is already in your repo; copy your database with pg_dump and your files with any S3 tool. Leave anytime →

Refunds

How refunds work for prepaid credits. Short version: unused credits come back to you.

Draft, not yet in effect. This page is waiting for legal review. Bracketed items will be filled in before it applies.

Effective [date set at launch] · Questions: support@inferralab.com

Unused credits

Ask within 14 days of a purchase and we'll refund the credits from that purchase that you haven't used.

Closing your account

To close your account, email support@inferralab.com from your account's address. We stop billing, refund your unused purchased credits, and delete your data 7 days later. The refund covers all unused purchased credits, whenever you bought them.

If we end the agreement

If we close your account for a breach of the terms, we refund unused credits unless the breach was fraud or abuse. If we shut Inferra down, we refund all unused purchased credits.

What isn't refundable

  • Usage that has already happened: the resources were reserved for you.
  • The $5 monthly allowance and any promotional credits: they're free usage, not money.

Outages

We don't offer a service-level agreement yet. If a problem on our side cost you credits, tell us; we'll review it and may add credits back as a goodwill gesture.

Card disputes

Please ask us first. A bank dispute is treated as a refund request, and the account may be paused while it's open.

How to ask

Email support@inferralab.com from your account's address with the purchase date. We reply within 5 business days. Refunds go back to the original card; if that card has expired, your bank usually forwards it, or we'll arrange another way. Banks typically take 5 to 10 business days to show the refund.

Subscription refunds

The $25 subscription payment is refunded on request within 7 days if usage was under $5. Usage already used is not refundable. Unused trial credit expires, and unused included usage does not roll over.

Cancellation

Cancel and service runs until the end of the paid month, then a 30-day hold, then deletion. Export data before the hold ends. Usage already used is not refundable; request a refund of the $25 within 7 days if usage was under $5. To request cancellation or a refund, email support@inferralab.com from your account’s address.

Card disputes

A card dispute stops the account. Contact support@inferralab.com first if a charge looks wrong.

How to ask

Email support@inferralab.com from your account’s address with the payment date. Do not send card details or credentials. See billing for trial expiry and payment failure terms.

Leave anytime

Your code, your data, your way out.

Nothing about Inferra locks you in. Here's how to take everything with you, whenever you want.

1 · Your code

It never left your GitHub repo. Inferra builds from it; it doesn't keep the only copy.

2 · Your configuration

.inferra/org.yaml and the branch files are in your repo too. Remove them whenever you like.

3 · Your database

Run pg_dump from an app bound to the database (Postgres is private, so it isn't reachable from your laptop). Copy the dump somewhere you control.

4 · Your files

Copy bucket contents with any S3 tool, such as aws s3 sync, from an app bound to the bucket. Apps reach buckets through their own identity; there are no keys to hand out.

5 · Your secrets

Secret values are write-only in Inferra, so they can't be read back. Keep the originals where you created them, such as GitHub environments or your password manager.

6 · Close the account

Email support@inferralab.com from your account's address. We stop billing, refund your unused purchased credits, and delete your data 7 days later. See Refunds.

Need help moving out? Contact us; we'll help either way.
